Known limits

  • Extraction is keyword-and-name matching, with no LLM: it finds sentences, then guesses the link type from the nearest keyword. The review column measures how often that guess is right.
  • Recall (relationships that exist but were missed) is not measured yet. Companies that only say "Customer A" can't be linked by name.
  • Revenue-share sizes exist for only a few links. Nearly all concentration disclosures are for unnamed customers ("two customers accounted for 19% and 15%"); only Entegris→TSMC (16%), KLA→TSMC (>10%) and Qualcomm→Apple/Samsung (≥10%) are named.
  • Some companies contributed no links from their own filing (0 in the "Links found" column below): their 10-Ks name few counterparties, or name them in ways the matcher doesn't catch.
  • Sector membership is hand-assigned; prices only measure how strongly each company follows it.
